## The two findings that decide the shape

**1. Engagement bait loses — by 2.3x on medians.**

CTA-baited posts (explicit ask for connections/comments): **median 2,450 views,
mean 3,133, max 6,100** (n=6).
No-CTA posts: **median 5,700, mean 36,535, max 366,000** (n=12).

Quote **2.3x**. The 40x figure is the best bait post against the best honest
post — a max-vs-max artefact of one outlier. The outlier is real and lives in
the mean column; that is where to report it.

**2. Copied bait templates get punished twice.**

@arjun_prmkmr and @dhairyadarji786 posted the same "building alone is brutal"
template. Measured similarity **0.88** — a copy *with edits*, not
word-for-word. Both got 1,900 views, ~3x below the original template's 6,100.

Say "0.88 similar" if you cite it. An earlier draft called it identical and was
wrong.

## Six patterns in the top four

1. **The first line is a verdict, not a topic.** "Lots of founders are crashing
   out." "AI automation is the HARDEST offer." Something you could disagree with.
2. **A number you can't fake, in the hook itself.** `$5,000/mo`, `6 leads in 3
   days`, `$1M ARR -> $5M in 15 months`.
3. **Number *plus cost*.** The 22K post opens with both: "…it took me 3 years &
   a burnout." Strongest shape available.
4. **Length is a choice.** 366K post ~1,600 chars; @gouthamjay8 ~900;
   @MaxGrant__ a bare list. **Nothing above 6K views is under 400 chars.**
5. **No CTA at all.** The two biggest posts ask for nothing. Bait gets
   1.9–6.1K; honesty gets 19–366K.
6. **Specific enemies, not categories.** "Everyone has access to the same
   tools." "Vibe coders cloning products without the care or craft." "From 3-4x
   revenue to 1x."

### What the 366K post actually contains

@helloitsolly — `https://x.com/i/status/2105292295935607045`. Verdict open, then
eight themes: AI marketing isn't working (everyone has the same tools, inboxes
flooded); engineers are fried (flow state gone, exhausted before 11am); "what's
the point?"; SaaS valuations 3-4x → 1x; more sellers than buyers; "a lot of
people trying to sell shovels"; build in public stopped being fun ("they are
influencers not founders"); "the challenges feel existential".

A **position, not a summary** — which is why it is arguable and therefore why it
spreads. No CTA, no offer, no link.

## The gap against our own pool

Our 23-file `x_pool/`: **avg 153 chars, zero numbers, zero named tools, zero
named people.**

Every post above 6K views is longer than anything in the pool, and every one
opens with a number. The pool leads with a *category* ("most AI tools just create
more decisions"); the winners lead with a *fact*. Same niche, different altitude.
That single difference is the reason for flat views.

## Citing these stats in our own post

Permitted **only with the method in the same sentence** — "I pulled 25 posts from
the top of this niche last month" is checkable; "7 of them opened with 'drop a
below'" is a fact we hold.

**Never adopt a competitor's number as our own metric.** The viral post built on
this file cites the 25 / 7 / 2,450 / 5,700 set and attributes the method, and
that is the shape to copy.

## The two posts worth studying as templates

- **@cbwritescopy** (19K): hard claim + specific niche + concrete result.
  "AI automation is the HARDEST offer for cold email. Yet 6 leads in 3 days
  targeting home service businesses (roofers, plumbers)."
- **@gouthamjay8** (22K): number **plus** cost. "$5,000/mo, 3 years, a burnout."

Both name a specific number and a specific cost. Neither asks for engagement.
