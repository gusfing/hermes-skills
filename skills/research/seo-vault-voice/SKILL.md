# SEO Vault Voice

> "A blank AI writes like the average of the internet; an agent that has read
> your last hundred pieces writes like you on a good day."
> — from the AI Profit Boardroom SEO guide

This is the glue. Skills are the hands, the vault is the taste.

## Where the vault lives

```
references/vault/
  voice.md      <- HOW they write. Sentence length, tone, punctuation habits.
  offers.md     <- WHAT they sell, and the exact phrasing they use.
  proof.md      <- verifiable results, client names, numbers
```

If `references/vault/` does not exist, **ask the user for three real past
samples and the core pitch in their own words.** Do not write the vault for
them. The vault's value is that it is *theirs*; synthesising it destroys the
only reason the skill exists.

## Procedure — before drafting anything

1. **Read all three vault files.** Every time. Not cached, not summarised.
2. **Extract the voice fingerprint:**
   - avg sentence length and the range
   - does it use fragments? contractions? em dashes?
   - does it open with a claim or a story?
   - banned words — anything they'd never say
3. **Check the offer.** Which of their offers does this content lead toward?
   Name it before drafting.
4. **Draft** in their voice.
5. **Self-check against the fingerprint.** Where does the draft drift?
   Rewrite those lines. A draft that needs "polishing" into their voice isn't
   finished.

## Output

Report: which offer this leads to, the three voice traits you matched, and any
line where you knowingly drifted and why.

## Hard rules

- **Never invent proof.** No fake metrics, no fake client names, no "increased
  revenue by 300%". If `proof.md` lacks a number, the content goes without it.
- **Never invent the vault.** Missing vault → ask. This is the one skill where
  "just write something reasonable" is strictly wrong.
- **Read before write, every run.** Skipping this is exactly how drafts end up
  sounding like the average of the internet.
